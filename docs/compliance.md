# Dealer record checks and limits

Sources checked 2026-10-06. This workflow is a consignment ledger. It does not determine whether a business needs a licence, whether a particular item is regulated, or whether external records satisfy the law. Set regulated for the actual item after checking the applicable category. OTHER jurisdictions need a local review.

## NZ

[NZ Police dealer guidance](https://www.police.govt.nz/advice-services/businesses-and-organisations/secondhand-dealers-and-pawnbrokers/secondhand-dealers) describes supplier identification, transaction and disposal records. Regulated items need the linked external dealer record and supplier identity reference. The check reports either missing reference. It cannot inspect the external evidence. Keep required records for three years. The usual fourteen-day retention rule has an agency-sale exception requiring purchaser details and identity verification. This app does not automatically claim that exception or decide a release date.

[NZ Police articles list](https://www.police.govt.nz/advice-services/businesses-and-organisations/secondhand-dealers-and-pawnbrokers/articles-defined) defines the categories to check. Clothing is not a blanket exemption: jackets, coats and leather clothing appear on that list.

## NSW, Australia

[NSW Government obligations](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/running-a-business/industry-specific-business-requirements/pawnbrokers-and-second-hand-dealers) describes identity evidence, electronic dealer records and reporting. Our checks flag missing references and always flag regulated NSW stock for external record keeping. Keep the approved dealer system and required Police transmission. This app is not approved dealer software. Consignment is an exception to the usual holding rule, not a blanket exemption from other obligations. Other Australian states need separate rules.

## Internal controls

TERMS means no signed agreement reference is recorded. Expiry and sixty-day ageing are business policies, not statutory deadlines. Donations require recorded permission. Sale recording requires a linked dealer record for regulated stock. The payout check prevents recording more than the available consignor credit. A full refund reverses that credit and can create a negative balance after a payout. These checks do not process money or replace tax accounting.

Keep sensitive identity documents in a restricted records system. This ledger holds references only. The operator manages database access, backups, retention and external evidence review. Printed statements and intake records are operational paperwork, not a substitute for the full statutory dealer record.
