# Why the consignor desk has no front end

The weekly questions are about records: what expires, what has sold, who is owed money and which agreement is missing. A coding agent reads the database and follows a command recipe. Read-only HTML reports and printable statements are enough for those reviews.

A staffed till needs fast scanning, receipts, card terminals and permissions. Mobile intake benefits from photos, offline work and touch controls. This free base does not provide those screens, live stock synchronisation, a consignor portal, email delivery or automatic payment processing. Keep those systems while moving the consignor ledger. Enterprise DNA can scope the connections and screens your store needs.

The free version runs with Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Cursor. The same instructions and database work with each. No runtime-specific fork is needed.
